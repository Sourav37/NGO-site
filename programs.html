OUR COMMUNITY PROGRAMS

Grassroots Action in West Bengal

Targeting education, healthcare, livelihoods, food security, and green action for lasting community dignity.

INITIATIVE 1

Project Shiksha: Child Education & Literacy

Our flagship educational program operates 12 evening learning centers across Kolkata urban slums and Sundarbans villages. We provide remedial coaching, digital literacy, school kits, and nutritious snacks.

Sponsor a Child's Education
INITIATIVE 2

Swayam Shakti: Women Skill Training

Empowering women with vocational sewing, embroidery, handicraft production, micro-finance self-help groups, and financial independence training across Bengal.

Volunteer as Skill Trainer
INITIATIVE 3

Arogya Mobile Health Unit

Deploying 3 custom mobile medical vans equipped with diagnostic gear, doctors, and essential medicines to remote Sundarbans island villages every week.

Support Medical Camps
INITIATIVE 4

Annapurna Food & Hunger Relief

Serving 500+ fresh hot meals daily to homeless elderly individuals and underprivileged school children in Kolkata street shelters.

Sponsor Community Meals
